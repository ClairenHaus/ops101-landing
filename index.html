Who this is for

This is for you if

  • You run a service business alone, or with a contractor or two.
  • You have never managed a team and nobody taught you how a business runs.
  • You want a plan you can start this week, not a lecture.

This is not for

  • Product, inventory or e-commerce businesses.
  • Companies that already have a management layer and a full ops team.
